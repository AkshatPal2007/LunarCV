About LunarCV

Problem Statement

LunarCV provides sub-pixel accurate, spatially uniform image registration between Chandrayaan-2 optical payloads (OHRC, TMC-2, IIRS) and reference imagery from LRO NAC and SELENE missions. The system handles three key challenges: illumination variation, viewpoint variation, and scale variation.

Technical Approach

Our pipeline combines LightGlue (learned feature matching), RIFT2 (phase congruency), MAGSAC++ (outlier rejection), grid-based spatial distribution enforcement, and sub-pixel refinement. This achieves better accuracy and uniformity than the published baseline while maintaining real-time performance.

Key Innovations

  • Cross-validation between learned and phase-based matchers
  • Explicit spatial uniformity enforcement (novel vs. baseline)
  • Geographic prior using mission metadata for faster convergence
  • Minimal preprocessing (avoiding overhead of classical methods)

Dataset Coverage

Real Chandrayaan-2 OHRC, TMC-2, and IIRS imagery paired with LRO NAC, LRO WAC, and SELENE reference data. Equatorial and polar regions. No synthetic/fabricated data in evaluation.
